BATCH 1: 500 GAME IDEAS FOR 3-10 PLAYERS (FOCUS: TROLLING ALLIES, SIMPLE RULES, MEMORABLE MOMENTS)

=== SOCIAL DEDUCTION / HIDDEN ROLE ===
1. "Traitor's Translator" - One player secretly retranslates everyone's words to the group with subtle malicious changes. Others must guess who's the "translator".
2. "Fake Rules" - Each round, one player invents a fake rule everyone must follow. Last to realize it's fake drinks/does penalty.
3. "Secret Signal" - Two players have a secret signal. They must communicate it to each other publicly without others noticing. Others bet on who the pair is.
4. "Gaslight Gallery" - Players describe a shared "memory" that never happened. One player wasn't there - they must pretend they remember. Group votes on the impostor.
5. "Whisper Down the Lane: Sabotage Edition" - Message passes through players. One designated saboteur must change exactly one word per pass. Final message read aloud.
6. "The Accuser" - One player accuses another of a ridiculous specific crime ("Dave stole my left sock at 3:47 PM"). Accused must defend with increasingly absurd evidence. Group votes guilty/not guilty.
7. "Two Truths, One Lie: Collaborative" - Group writes 3 statements about one player. That player must guess which is the lie. If wrong, they do a dare.
8. "Poker Face Poker" - Play poker but you can verbally declare any hand. Others choose to believe or call bluff. No cards shown until end.
9. "The Mole's Notebook" - One player has a notebook of "secret missions" (e.g., "make someone say 'banana'"). They read missions aloud but assign them to OTHER players secretly.
10. "Impostor Syndrome" - Everyone gets a role card. One says "Impostor". Impostor doesn't know they're the impostor. Others must guide them to reveal themselves without saying it directly.
11. "Blind Accusation" - Lights out. One player tapped as "killer". Lights on. Dead player gives ONE vague clue. Group votes. Killer wins if survives 3 rounds.
12. "Fake Identity" - Each player draws a celebrity/character. They answer questions AS that person. One player has NO card - they improvise. Group guesses who's faking.
13. "The Frame-Up" - Before game, each player writes a "crime" on paper. Shuffle. Each round, one crime read. Players accuse each other. Real culprit stays silent.
14. "Paranoia Circle" - Whisper a question to neighbor ("Who here would survive a horror movie?"). Neighbor answers NAME OUT LOUD. Coin flip: if heads, question revealed. If tails, stays secret.
15. "Liar's Resume" - Each player writes 3 fake qualifications for a ridiculous job ("Professional Cloud Taster"). Read aloud. Group hires one. Hired player must justify qualifications for 60 seconds.
16. "Secret Santa Sabotage" - Draw names. Buy/give "gifts" (can be trash, insults, rocks). Recipient must act grateful and guess giver. Giver can lie.
17. "The Witness" - One player witnesses a "crime" (staged by host). They describe it BADLY on purpose. Others reconstruct. Witness gets points for each wrong detail others include.
18. "Alibi" - Pair up. 5 mins to create shared alibi for a ridiculous crime. Separated. Interrogated individually. Contradictions = guilt.
19. "Trust Fall: Verbal" - Player states "I trust [name] with [secret/action]". That person can betray or honor. Points for honor, double for betrayal undetected.
20. "The Plant" - One player given a ridiculous opinion to defend ("Hot dogs are sandwiches"). Others don't know who. Plant gets points for each person they convince.
21. "Mafia: Narrator Lies" - Standard Mafia but narrator secretly changes results. "Doctor saved you" (didn't). "Investigator found mafia" (wrong). Players must detect narrator betrayal.
22. "Hidden Agenda" - Each player gets a secret win condition ("Make someone laugh", "Get voted out", "Say 'actually' 5 times"). Game ends when someone achieves theirs.
23. "The Informant" - One player gets all roles secretly. They announce "I'm the informant" and give ONE piece of true info, rest lies. Others must parse.
24. "Gaslighting: The Game" - Players take turns stating "Remember when [X] happened?" Group must agree or disagree. One player was asleep/absent for real events.
25. "Spyfall: Broken Translator" - Spy gets a translator that gives wrong location names. Spy must guess real location from bad translations.

=== PHYSICAL / DEXTERITY / PARTY ===

31. "Human Knot: Sabotage" - Form knot. One player secretly told "don't let it untangle". They subtly resist. Group must identify saboteur.
32. "Egg Roulette" - 12 eggs: 8 boiled, 4 raw. Players take turns smashing on forehead. Raw = out. Can swap eggs secretly.
33. "Sock Puppet Charades" - Put socks on hands. Act out movie titles. Socks have googly eyes. Must use funny voices.
34. "Blind Taste Test: Prison Edition" - Blindfolded. Fed baby food, condiments, weird combos. Guess what it is. Wrong = penalty.
35. "Balloon Pop Butt" - Balloons tied to ankles. Pop others' while protecting yours. Last balloon wins. Can form alliances then betray.
36. "Mummy Wrap Race" - Teams wrap one player in toilet paper. "Mummy" must then complete obstacle course. Paper tears = penalty.
37. "Cookie Face" - Cookie on forehead. Move to mouth using only facial muscles. No hands. Fail = eat off floor.
38. "Spoon Race: Raw Egg" - Standard but eggs raw. Can bump others' spoons. "Accidental" bumps encouraged.
39. "Blindfolded Drawing" - Draw object described by teammate. Teammate CANNOT name object, only describe shapes. Drawer blindfolded.
40. "Human Chair" - Players form circle, sit on each other's laps simultaneously. One chair removed each round. Betrayal = push neighbor.
41. "Nose Pencil" - Pencil tied to string on waist. Put in bottle using only hip movements. Others shake bottle.
42. "Cheese Puff Face" - Cover face in shaving cream. Throw cheese puffs. Stick = points. Thrower aims for face holes.
43. "Saran Wrap Ball" - Ball of saran wrap with prizes inside. Unwrap with oven mitts. Dice passes turn. Can steal ball.
44. "Pickle Juice Pong" - Beer pong but pickle juice. Make shot = opponent drinks. Miss = you drink double.
45. "Blindfolded Haircut" - One cuts another's hair (wig or real) blindfolded. Third guides (can sabotage). Reveal at end.
46. "Toilet Paper Bride" - Teams make wedding dress from TP. Model walks runway. Judge = player who didn't participate. Sabotage allowed.
47. "Donut on String" - Donuts hanging. Eat hands-free. String length adjusted by opponents mid-game.
48. "Wet Sponge Relay" - Pass sponge over head/under legs. Squeeze into bucket. Can squeeze on teammates instead.
49. "Face the Cookie" - Oreo on forehead. Wiggle to mouth. First to eat wins. Others can blow on face.
50. "Human Bowling" - One player on scooter/skateboard. Others roll them at pins (bottles). Steering by teammates shouting.

BATCH 16: 500 GAME IDEAS FOR 3-10 PLAYERS (FOCUS: TROLLING ALLIES, SIMPLE RULES, MEMORABLE MOMENTS)

=== NEW CATEGORY: NARRATIVE/STORYTELLING GAMES (FINAL EXPANSION) ===
7501. "Collaborative Novel: One Sentence" - Each player writes ONE sentence. Pass. Next writes next. "Dave woke up." "He was a toaster." "Sarah ate him." "Dave: 'WHAT.'" Read final aloud.
7502. "Exquisite Corpse Story" - Fold paper. Write 2 sentences. Fold so only last shows. Pass. Next continues. Unfold. "Dave found a lamp. Genie granted wish. Dave wished for naps. Genie: 'Done.' Dave napped forever. Sarah: 'My turn.'"
7503. "Genre Shift" - Start story in one genre. Every 3 sentences, NEXT player shifts genre. "Noir detective..." → "Space opera!" → "Romance!" → "Horror!" → "Cooking show!"
7504. "Character Assassination" - Create character together. Each adds ONE trait. "Dave: Brave." "Sarah: But terrified of spoons." "Mike: And secretly a spoon." "Jen: Who collects spoons." Result: tragic hero.
7505. "Obituary Improv" - One player = recently deceased. Others give eulogies. "Dave died doing what he loved: nothing." "He is survived by: 47 hoodies, his dignity (missing), and a gym membership (unused)."
7506. "Fake History" - Invent historical event. "The Great Hoodie War of 2019." Each adds detail. "Dave led the rebellion." "Sarah negotiated peace." "Mike sold weapons to both sides." "Jen documented it all."
7507. "Urban Legend Builder" - Start: "They say if you..." Each adds clause. "They say if you say 'Dave's on his way' 3 times in a mirror..." "He appears..." "But he's late..." "And asks for a ride."
7508. "Conspiracy Board" - Connect random things. "Dave's gym absence" → "Linked to" → "Alien abduction" → "Because" → "He was probing" → "Actually" → "He was napping." Red string everywhere.
7509. "Wiki Article: Fake" - Write fake Wikipedia entry together. "Dave Smith (born 1990) is a professional napper. Known for: '5 minutes' = 45 minutes. Awards: 'Most Likely to Ghost' (2019-2024)."
7510. "Choose Your Own Adventure: Group" - One writes scenario. Group votes on choice. "Dave finds a mysterious door." A) Open. B) Nap. C) Call Sarah. Vote. Next writes outcome.

=== NEW CATEGORY: SECRET ROLE/PERSONALITY GAMES (FINAL EXPANSION) ===
7511. "Secret Quirk" - Each gets secret quirk card: "Must rhyme." "Speak in questions." "Refer to self in third person." "Touch nose before speaking." Play normal game while maintaining quirk.
7512. "Hidden Agenda" - Each gets secret win condition: "Make Dave laugh." "Get voted out." "Say 'banana' 7 times." "Convince someone you're a wizard." Game ends when someone achieves theirs.
7513. "Personality Swap" - Draw another player's name. Must act like them ALL GAME. "I'm Dave now. *naps* '5 minutes.' *wakes* 'I'm late.'" Real Dave: "THAT'S NOT ME." "IT IS NOW."
7514. "Impostor Syndrome" - Everyone gets role card. One says "Impostor." Impostor doesn't know they're impostor. Others must guide them to reveal themselves without saying it directly.
7515. "Gaslighter" - One player = Gaslighter. Their job: make others doubt reality. "That never happened." "You're imagining things." "Dave, you weren't even there." Points for each person who agrees.
7516. "The Plant" - One player given ridiculous opinion to defend ("Hot dogs are sandwiches"). Others don't know who. Plant gets points for each person they convince.
7517. "Secret Signal" - Two players have secret signal. They must communicate it publicly without others noticing. Others bet on who the pair is. Pair wins if undetected.
7518. "Double Agent" - Two spies, each thinks they're the only one. Handler gives conflicting missions. Spies must identify each other while completing missions.
7519. "Triple Agent" - Three players: Loyalist, Traitor, Double Agent. Double Agent wins with whoever wins. Loyalist/Traitor don't know Double Agent exists.
7520. "The Mole's Notebook" - One player has notebook of "secret missions" (e.g., "make someone say 'banana'"). They read missions aloud but assign them to OTHER players secretly.

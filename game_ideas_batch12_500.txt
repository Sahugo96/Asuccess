=== NEW CATEGORY: SOCIAL EXPERIMENTS/PSYCHOLOGICAL ===
5601. "The Trust Game" - Pair up. Share secret. Other can: Keep, Share with group, Use against you. Reveal choices. Discuss.
5602. "Prisoner's Dilemma: Party" - Pair. Secret: Cooperate/Betray. Both C = both win small. Both B = both lose. One C one B = B wins big, C loses big.
5603. "Ultimatum Game: Social" - Proposer splits $10 (fake). Responder accepts/rejects. Reject = both get 0. Rotate. Discuss fairness.
5604. "Dictator Game: Personal" - Dictator splits $10. Responder NO choice. Must accept. Dictator rotates. Observe generosity. Discuss.
5605. "Public Goods: Group" - Each puts 0-10 in pot. Pot doubled. Split equally. Free rider problem. Repeat. Discuss cooperation.
5606. "Beauty Contest: Keynesian" - Pick number 0-100. Winner = closest to 2/3 average. Iterate reasoning. "I think they think I think..."
5607. "Traveler's Dilemma" - Both claim value $2-$100. Lower gets +$2, higher -$2. Equal = both get value. Nash equilibrium = $2. Play.
5608. "Volunteer's Dilemma" - One must volunteer (cost 5). All benefit (10). If none volunteer, all get 0. Who sacrifices? Repeat.
5609. "War of Attrition" - Bid time (0-100). Higher wins prize (100). Both pay bid. Longest wins. Escalation. "I'll wait longer."
5610. "Dollar Auction" - Auction $1. Both highest bidders PAY. Second place loses bid. Escalation past $1. "I'm already in for $20..."
5611. "Stag Hunt" - Two hunters. Stag = high reward (need both). Hare = low reward (solo). Trust vs safety. Play repeatedly.
5612. "Chicken Game" - Two drivers. Swerve = lose face. Straight = crash. Who blinks? Play with social stakes.
5613. "Centipede Game" - Pass = small reward now. Continue = potential bigger later. But other can take at any point. Backwards induction.
5614. "Guess 2/3 Average" - Pick 0-100. Closest to 2/3 mean wins. Level-k thinking. "Everyone picks 50 → 33 → 22 → 15 → 10 → 7 → 5 → 3 → 2 → 1 → 0."
5615. "Common Knowledge" - Public announcement changes private knowledge. "At least one has blue eyes." Logic puzzle as game.
5616. "Muddy Children" - Father: "At least one muddy." Children: "Don't know." Repeat. Eventually all know. Perfect induction.
5617. "Sum and Product" - Two numbers. Sum to one, product to other. Dialogue: "Don't know." "Knew you didn't." "Now know." "Now know too."
5618. "Cheryl's Birthday" - 10 dates. Albert knows month, Bernard knows day. Dialogue eliminates. Logic puzzle as social game.
5619. "Blue Eyes Islander" - 100 islanders. Guru: "I see blue eyes." All leave night 100. Common knowledge induction.
5620. "Unexpected Hanging" - Judge: "Hanging next week, unexpected." Prisoner: "Can't be Friday..." Paradox. Discuss.
5621. "Surprise Exam" - Teacher: "Exam next week, surprise." Students: "Can't be Friday..." Paradox. When is it?
5622. "Heap Paradox" - 1 grain = not heap. n grains = heap if n-1 = heap. When does heap start? Vagueness game.
5623. "Ship of Theseus" - Replace planks one by one. Same ship? Replace all = new ship? Original planks rebuilt = original?
5624. "Teleporter Paradox" - Destroy body, recreate elsewhere. Same person? What if original not destroyed? Two copies?
5625. "Brain in a Vat" - You're a brain. Experiences simulated. How prove otherwise? Skepticism as game.
5626. "Experience Machine" - Machine gives perfect life. Plug in? Reality vs happiness. Nozick's thought experiment.
5627. "Utility Monster" - One being gets 1000x utility from resources. Others get 1x. Utilitarianism says feed monster. Fairness?
5628. "Repugnant Conclusion" - Huge population barely happy > small population very happy. Parfit's paradox. Population ethics.
5629. "Non-Identity Problem" - Actions change who exists. Can't harm future people (they wouldn't exist otherwise). Ethics.
5630. "Mere Addition" - Add happy people. World better? But leads to repugnant conclusion. Paradox chain.
5631. "Procreation Asymmetry" - Creating miserable life = bad. Creating happy life = not good (no one deprived). Asymmetry.
5632. "Future Tuesday Indifference" - Pain on Tuesday vs pain today. Should care equally? Time bias. Rationality.
5633. "Self-Torturer" - Device: 1000 settings. Each imperceptibly worse. $10,000 per step. Rational to continue? Sorites.
5634. "Money Pump" - Intransitive preferences: A>B, B>C, C>A. Exploited for money. Rationality requires transitivity.
5635. "Allais Paradox" - Certainty effect. 1A vs 1B, 2A vs 2B. Violates expected utility. Human irrationality.
5636. "Ellsberg Paradox" - Ambiguity aversion. Known vs unknown probabilities. Violates subjective expected utility.
5637. "Preference Reversal" - Price vs choice. Choose safe, price risky. Inconsistent. Context dependence.
5638. "Endowment Effect" - Own mug = value $7. Don't own = pay $3. Loss aversion. Ownership changes value.
5639. "Status Quo Bias" - Default option chosen. Even if worse. Inertia. "Do nothing" as decision.
5640. "Loss Aversion" - Lose $100 hurts 2x more than gain $100 feels good. Kahneman-Tversky. Prospect theory.
5641. "Anchoring" - Random number influences estimate. Wheel of fortune → UN African nations. Irrelevant anchor.
5642. "Availability Heuristic" - Vivid examples = overestimate probability. Plane crashes vs car crashes. Media bias.
5643. "Representativeness" - Linda: bank teller + feminist > bank teller. Conjunction fallacy. Stereotypes.
5644. "Base Rate Neglect" - Cab: 85% green, 15% blue. Witness: 80% reliable says blue. Actual probability? 41% blue.
5645. "Conjunction Fallacy" - "Linda is bank teller AND feminist" > "Linda is bank teller". Impossible but feels true.
5646. "Planning Fallacy" - Estimates always optimistic. "Two weeks" → two months. Reference class forecasting.
5647. "Optimism Bias" - "Bad things happen to others." 80% think above average driver. Statistical impossibility.
5648. "Confirmation Bias" - Seek confirming evidence. Ignore disconfirming. Wason selection task. 4 cards: E, K, 4, 7.
5649. "Hindsight Bias" - "Knew it all along." Outcome known → probability inflated. Memory reconstruction.
5650. "Overconfidence" - 90% confidence intervals contain true value 50% time. Calibration training.

BATCH 2: 500 GAME IDEAS FOR 3-10 PLAYERS (FOCUS: TROLLING ALLIES, SIMPLE RULES, MEMORABLE MOMENTS)

=== SOCIAL DEDUCTION / HIDDEN ROLE (CONTINUED) ===
501. "Double Agent" - Two spies, each thinks they're the only one. Handler gives conflicting missions. Spies must identify each other while completing missions.
502. "Triple Agent" - Three players: Loyalist, Traitor, Double Agent. Double Agent wins with whoever wins. Loyalist/Traitor don't know Double Agent exists.
503. "Quadruple Agent" - Four layers. Each player gets a card: "Loyal", "Traitor", "Double", "Triple". Each beats the one below. Rock-paper-scissors loyalty.
504. "The Mole: Journal Swap" - Mole's journal passes to random player each round. New holder can continue sabotages or expose. Mole rotates? No, Mole fixed, journal moves.
505. "Traitors: Recruitment" - Traitors can recruit Faithful at night. Recruited becomes Traitor. Faithful don't know numbers. Banish vote daily.
506. "Clue: Liar's Dice" - One player lies about their cards shown. Others deduce. Accuse liar. Wrong accusation = accuser out. Liar wins if undetected.
507. "Guess Who: Multiplayer Elimination" - Each player has secret character. Ask yes/no questions to group. Eliminate. Last unidentified wins. Can lie once per game.
508. "Battleship: Fog of War" - Ships placed. Shots called. Opponent says "hit/miss" but CAN LIE once per ship. Radar reveals 3x3 area once per game.
509. "Connect 4: Gravity Shift" - Standard but board rotates 90° every 3 turns. Pieces fall new direction. Plan for rotation.
510. "Chess: Fog of War" - See only squares your pieces attack. Move into unknown. Scout pieces reveal. Bluff attacks.
511. "Poker: Community Cards" - Texas Hold'em but community cards revealed ONE AT A TIME with betting between. Can "peek" next card for fee.
512. "Mafia: Day/Night Swap" - Day: public discussion. Night: secret actions. But roles swap each phase: Day Mafia, Night Villagers. Chaos.
513. "Werewolf: Amnesia" - Roles assigned. Night 1: everyone wakes, sees ONE other role, forgets own. Must deduce from that glimpse.
514. "Resistance: Blind Missions" - Mission team selected. Cards: Success/Fail/REVERSE. Reverse flips outcome. Spies know Reverse cards. Resistance doesn't.
515. "Spyfall: Time Limit" - 5 min timer. Spy must guess location. Non-spies must guess spy. Accusations at any time. Wrong accusation = spy wins.
516. "Codenames: Assassin's Creed" - Assassin word = instant loss. But Spymaster can give "meta-clues" about assassin location. "Avoid: 2, 3, 1".
517. "Decrypto: Double Agent" - Each team has a mole. Mole gets both code decks. Must help own team while leaking to other. Identify moles.
518. "Just One: Saboteur Known" - Saboteur known to all. Writes clue. Others write clues. Guesser knows one clue is bad. Which one?
519. "So Clover: Adversarial" - Teams. Each team writes clues for SAME keywords. Other team reconstructs. Better reconstruction wins. Sabotage clues.
520. "Crossfire: Double Sniper" - Two snipers. Two VIPs. Agents protect both. Assassins kill both. Snipers shoot simultaneously. Complex resolution.

=== PHYSICAL / DEXTERITY / PARTY (CONTINUED) ===

522. "Blindfolded Trust Walk" - Pairs. One blindfolded. Other guides verbally. Can guide into walls, other pairs, "lava" (floor). Switch.

525. "Statues: Museum" - One "guard". Others = statues. Move when guard not looking. Guard catches movement = out. Guard can't turn instantly.

527. "Mother May I: Sabotage" - "Mother" grants/denies steps. Can grant weird steps: "3 hops backward while clapping". Players can appeal to "Father" (other player).

529. "Telephone: Draw" - Draw on back of next player. They draw on next. Final drawing vs original. No talking.
530. "Charades: Reverse" - Guesser acts. Team gives clues. "You're a... tree? No, toaster!" Guesser must guess from team's clues.

536. "Most Likely To: Evidence" - Vote. Target must provide "evidence" (story, scar, text) or drink. Group judges evidence quality.
537. "Paranoia: Whisper Chain" - Whisper question. Answer names person. That person whispers NEW question to next. Chain continues. Final question revealed.
